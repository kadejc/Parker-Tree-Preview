Decades of Trusted Tree Care

Brownie Parker founded Parker Tree Service in Bakersfield, California, in 1937. He climbed and trimmed trees over 250 feet tall until the age of 63, passing down the techniques we still practice today—techniques aligned with the principles taught through ISA arborist certification.

His son, George, brought that tradition to DFW, building a crew with decades of experience and a commitment to educating and caring for every client.

After George’s untimely passing, his right-hand man, Ryder, committed to carrying the Parker family legacy forward. Today, we honor that legacy by preserving the craft of tree trimming and doing it the right way, not the easy way.
