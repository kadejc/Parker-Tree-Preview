Tree rules in Plano

Plano’s landscaping and tree preservation rules sit in Article 17 of its Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance. They mainly apply to development and site plans; check with the city before removing a tree on a property under development or review.

Oncor, not the city or the homeowner, trims trees that grow into overhead power lines in Plano. Never prune near a line yourself; call Oncor for line clearance and call us for the rest of the tree.

Sources: City of Plano: Urban Forestry; City of Plano: Oncor Tree Trimming. City rules change; confirm with the City of Plano before removing a tree.

Local tree notes

Plano is home to the Quincentennial Bur Oak in Bob Woodruff Park, recognized in 1987 as having stood here since the signing of the U.S. Constitution. Bur oaks, live oaks, cedar elms, and pecans like it are worth protecting with careful pruning rather than heavy cuts.
