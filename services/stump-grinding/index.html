Stump Grinding

Remove the stump below ground level and reclaim usable space in your yard.

A tree may be gone, but the stump it leaves behind can still get in the way. Whether it’s a reminder of a recent removal or something you’ve been mowing around for years, stump grinding helps you reclaim that part of your yard.

We grind the stump below ground level, removing the visible obstacle and making the area easier to plan around. Before we begin, we discuss access, nearby features, and how you want to use the space so you know what to expect.

From a single stubborn stump to the finishing touch on a larger removal, we bring the same care and attention we give every tree on your property. Our goal is simple: help you move forward with a yard you can enjoy.

What should we discuss before grinding?

Let us know how you plan to use the area. We can discuss access, nearby features, the remaining grindings and any plans for replanting before the work is agreed.

What happens next

  1. Tell us about your trees

    Text or call with your city and what you need. Not sure which service? Start with what you’re seeing.

  2. Discuss the work and price

    We’ll talk through the next step and arrange an assessment when needed.

  3. Approve and schedule

    Once you’re ready, we’ll arrange the work with our crew.
