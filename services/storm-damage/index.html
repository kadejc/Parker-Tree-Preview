Storm Damage

24/7 emergency service for storm-damaged, split, uprooted and fallen trees, with help documenting tree damage for insurance claims.

When a storm brings down a tree, splits a trunk, or leaves a broken limb hanging over your roof, call us at (214) 394-2414. Our emergency tree service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for homeowners, HOAs, and commercial properties.

We handle trees that have fallen on houses, fences, cars, and driveways; hanging and broken limbs; and uprooted or dangerously leaning trees. A storm-damaged tree is often under tension or resting on a structure, so the order of cuts matters. We plan each step to bring it down without adding to the damage.

We also help you document the tree damage for your insurance claim, including photos of the damage and a written description of the tree work for your adjuster. Your insurance company decides what your policy covers, so contact them early.

Many storm failures start with problems that thoughtful pruning can address before the next storm: heavy branch ends, weak or crossing branches, and deadwood.

Questions about storm-damaged trees

Do you offer emergency tree removal after a storm?

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency tree service for storm-damaged, split, uprooted, and fallen trees. Call (214) 394-2414 any time.

A tree fell on my house. What should I do?

Get everyone out of the damaged area and stay away from the tree. If power lines are down or touching the tree, keep clear and call 911 and your electric provider first. Then call us for emergency tree service, take photos of the damage for your insurance company, and do not try to cut a tree that is under tension or resting on the structure.

Will insurance pay for tree removal after a storm?

It depends on your policy and what the tree damaged. Homeowners policies often cover removing a tree that fell on an insured structure, such as your house or a fence, and often do not cover a tree that fell without damaging anything. Your insurance company makes that decision. We help you document the tree damage for your claim.

What should I photograph for my insurance claim?

Before anything is moved, and only from a safe distance, photograph the whole tree, where it landed, every damaged part of the house, fence, or car, and the broken trunk or root plate. Wide shots plus close-ups help. We add photos and a written description of the tree work we do.

Do you handle storm cleanup for HOAs and commercial properties?

Yes. We clear storm-damaged and fallen trees from HOA common areas, apartment and townhome communities, and commercial properties, including trees blocking drives, walkways, and parking areas.

Can pruning help prevent storm damage?

Often, yes. Reducing weight at the branch ends, removing deadwood, and correcting weak or crossing branches helps a canopy handle wind. It cannot make any tree storm-proof, and a tree with root or trunk decay may need a different decision.
