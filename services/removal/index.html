Questions about tree removal

Should I trim or remove my tree?

We prefer to help a tree recover when that is a practical option. Trimming or treatment may be enough when the trunk and roots are sound; removal makes sense when a tree is dead, structurally failing, or a hazard that pruning cannot fix. An assessment helps us explain the options honestly.

Can you remove a large tree in a tight space?

Our crew has experience with large trees near homes and removals without an open drop zone. The tree’s condition, access, and nearby structures determine the approach.

How much does tree removal cost?

Every removal is priced individually. The tree’s size, condition, nearby structures, and access all matter. We discuss the scope and price with you before the work is agreed.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Plano or Dallas?

It depends on the city, the tree, and the property. Plano, Dallas, and other North Texas cities have tree ordinances that mainly apply to protected trees, development, and commercial sites, and HOAs may have their own rules. Check with your city and HOA before removal; we are happy to talk through what we know.
