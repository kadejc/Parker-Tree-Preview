Feeding

Targeted iron and zinc nutrition to help trees affected by nutrient deficiencies.

Iron and zinc, where your tree needs them

Sometimes the nutrients a tree needs are present in the soil but difficult for its roots to absorb. Alkaline soil can limit the availability of iron and zinc, contributing to pale foliage and poor growth.

Our certified injectors use a specialized micronutrient formulation designed to correct iron and zinc deficiencies. Trunk injections deliver nutrients directly into the tree’s vascular system, bypassing the soil conditions that can limit uptake.

What this treatment can help address

Iron deficiency

Yellowing leaves with green veins can be a sign of iron deficiency, especially in newer growth.

Zinc deficiency

Small leaves, yellowing foliage, and stunted growth can be signs of zinc deficiency.

Similar symptoms can have different causes. We assess your tree before recommending feeding, so the treatment fits the problem.
