Trimming Methodology

Fine Tip Pruning

Small, precise cuts reduce weight at the branch tips, allowing the canopy to spring upward. We guide growth while preserving healthy interior foliage and the tree’s natural shape.

Reduced weight at the branch tips while preserving the tree’s natural shape.

How We Trim

Structural Pruning

We remove problem branches and deadwood while preserving healthy interior growth. This helps keep weight closer to the trunk and encourages more uniform growth.

Every branch has a purpose

  • Keep healthy interior foliage.
  • Address rubbing, crossing and poorly directed growth.
  • Remove deadwood while preserving the tree’s natural form.
How We Prune

Clearance From Structures

We create clearance around your home, nearby trees, and streets while keeping overhanging branches lighter. Proper pruning helps protect your property and preserve tree health.
